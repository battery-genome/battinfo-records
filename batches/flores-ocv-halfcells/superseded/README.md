# Superseded records

Records this batch has replaced. They are kept, not deleted, because their published copies are still live and the repo convention is to "preserve old source references when they remain historically relevant, and note what has been superseded" (`docs/versioning-and-revisions.md`).

Nothing in this directory is regenerated by `build_records.py` or `build_bundle.py`. `build_bundle.py` rebuilds `records/` from scratch on every run, which is exactly why the retired files were moved out of it.

## v1 - the material layer, retired by the electrode remodel

`v1/material-spec/` (9) and `v1/material/` (12) are the records corpus v1 wrote before BattINFO had first-class electrodes. They are retired by BIG-MAP/BattINFO#342: a material spec describes a **powder**, and none of these did.

- The nine v1 `material-spec` records were *electrode products* (active-material kind x electrode source). They are replaced by 12 `electrode-spec` records, one per electrode design, plus one genuine `material-spec` for the only powder the source identifies (the high Mn/Ni disorder LNMO, `spec/epzg-hf4v-k5bk-nqxe`).
- The twelve v1 `material` records were *coated electrode batches*, carrying the aqueous/NMP processing route as an instance property. They are replaced 1:1 by `electrode` records, with the route moved to the electrode spec where it belongs: for an electrode the route is a design decision, so it is part of the spec identity.

## What happens to the published copies

All 21 are live in the Battery Genome registry today and stay live. Retiring them is part of the republish, not of this branch:

1. Publish the 25 new records (1 material spec, 12 electrode specs, 12 electrodes), specs before instances.
2. Update the 9 cell specs in place - same IRIs, new electrode references.
3. Mark the 21 records here superseded, pointing at the replacement IRIs in the tables above.

Steps 1-3 are a single reviewed operation. Until it runs, the registry copy of this dataset is the v1 shape and the branch is the v2 proposal.
